# Thinking skills

Skills that make you think harder: about your own beliefs, or about a topic you want to learn. Each folder holds one skill in a `SKILL.md`, and each works in any folder: no vault or conventions file needed.

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `argue` | Cross-examines a claim you hold: asks why you believe it, names your biases, then has you argue the other side or counters you itself. Saves a ledger of the points that held, the flaws you conceded, and what is still open. |
| `feynman` | Learns a topic by having you teach it to a curious novice: maps it into concepts, challenges every term you don't unpack, and climbs a hint ladder when you're stuck instead of giving the answer. Optionally saves a ledger of your explanations, the gaps you found, and what is still open. |

Invoked explicitly by name.
